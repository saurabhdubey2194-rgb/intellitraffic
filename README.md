IntelliTraffic is an intelligent traffic and emergency response platform designed for Delhi NCR, especially Delhi and Noida, that connects users, verified ambulances, police stations, and hospitals through one unified system.

For everyday users, IntelliTraffic provides an intelligent route-planning system that analyzes traffic density, congestion, signal delays, road conditions, and estimated travel time to recommend the fastest and least-congested route. Users can also explore live traffic conditions, traffic signals, accidents, road closures, nearby hospitals, and police stations through an interactive map.

For emergency services, an ambulance can register its vehicle and driver details, upload documents such as RC, permit, insurance, and authorization, and submit them for police verification. Once verified, the ambulance can request an emergency green corridor. IntelliTraffic then simulates intelligent signal optimization along the emergency route, prioritizing signals based on traffic conditions and ambulance movement instead of relying only on fixed 60-second timings.

Police authorities can verify ambulance documents, approve or reject emergency requests, monitor active emergencies, and manage green-corridor operations. Hospitals form the final stage of the workflow by confirming ambulance arrival and completing the emergency trip.

The platform also includes a History & Activity Control System that securely records important platform activities such as user logins, route searches, ambulance registrations, document verification, police approvals, emergency trips, green-corridor activation, hospital arrival confirmation, and system events.
